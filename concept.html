Our Start-to-Finish Master Plan

Here is how every piece of Aqeelah's Beauty Empire fits together in Cape Town (CPT) and Johannesburg (JHB), from the first client booking to our long-term vision.

Stage 1 • The Polish Drop

The Visual Contract: turning every nail photo into a guaranteed booking.

Part 1 • The Core Vision

The Visual Contract: Killing the "Inspo vs. Reality" Gap

Right now in South Africa, booking nails is a gamble. A client finds a pretty photo on Pinterest, sends it on WhatsApp, waits hours for a reply, and still does not know if the nail tech can actually pull that look off or what the final bill will be.

How we fix it: On Aqeelah's Beauty Empire, a nail tech's portfolio is not just a gallery. Every photo is a Visual Contract.

  • What You See Is What You Pay Every portfolio photo has its exact base price and appointment duration locked right onto the image. No awkward 20-minute WhatsApp price negotiations.
  • Simple Add-On Switches Need a soak-off or extra length? The client simply taps a toggle switch before paying, and the app automatically adds the right amount of money and extra minutes to the booking.
  • Launching with Nails in CPT & JHB We start focused exclusively on Nails across Cape Town (led by Aqeelah) and Johannesburg (led by Rio) before expanding to Wigs and Lashes later.
Part 2 • Safety & The Empty Chair Model

Strictly Verified Salons Only (Zero House Calls)

To protect both clients and nail techs, and to keep our brand reputation spotless, we do not allow house calls or home visits on the app.

Instead, every single booking happens inside a safe, professional commercial space through two options:

  • Option 1: The Tech's Verified Studio Chair If an independent nail tech already rents a chair or owns a commercial studio, we verify their location and clients book them there.
  • Option 2: Partner Salon "Empty Chairs" Many independent nail bars in CPT and JHB have quiet hours during the week (like Tuesday to Thursday mornings) where their stations sit empty while they still pay shop rent. We partner with those salons so our verified techs can use an idle chair for a booking, and the salon earns a guaranteed chair fee. Everybody wins.
Part 3 • Quality & Merit Badges

Free-Market Pricing + Aqeelah's "Certified Pro" Seal

Nail techs can set their own prices based on their skill and demand, while our badge system tells clients exactly who they can trust:

  • 1. Rising Tech Clean entry badge for newly onboarded techs whose identity and salon venue have been verified.
  • 2. Verified Tech Unlocked automatically after completing a set number of bookings with high client ratings and clear completion photos.
  • 3. Certified Pro (The Gold Standard) Our highest seal of quality. If a nail tech is already super talented and wants to jump straight to the top tier without waiting for 50 bookings, Aqeelah grades their work directly (via a live demo or model inspection) to award the Certified Pro badge.
Part 4 • Anti-Fraud & Dispute Protection

How an Appointment Works on the Day (PIN + Live Camera)

We protect both the client and the nail tech from scams, account sharing, and unfair complaints with a simple two-step check:

  • Start of Appointment: FaceID + Randomised 4-Digit Client PIN When the client sits in the chair, their phone shows a unique 4-digit PIN for that booking. The tech scans their FaceID/selfie and types the client's 4-digit PIN into the app to start the timer. This proves the real tech and the real client are together.
  • End of Appointment: Live In-App Camera Photo To mark the job finished and unlock their payout, the tech snaps a live photo of the completed nails using our in-app camera (old gallery uploads are blocked).
  • 30-Second Dispute Resolution If a client ever claims their nails do not look like what they booked, we simply look at three photos side-by-side: (1) The Booked Portfolio Photo, (2) The Tech's Live Completion Photo, and (3) The Client's Photo. If the tech delivered poor work, we credit the client's wallet fairly.
Part 5 • The Money Engine

Upfront Payments, The Wallet & Fair Cancellations

Every booking is paid 100% upfront (via Card, Instant EFT, or PayJustNow Buy-Now-Pay-Later). The money sits safely in our system until the appointment is completed.

  • 15% Platform Fee & Weekly Tech Payouts We take a 15% platform fee per booking. Techs get paid out automatically every week (or every two weeks), with an option to cash out instantly for a small fee whenever they need quick cash.
  • What Happens If a Client Cancels Late? (75% / 25% Rule) If a client cancels late, the nail tech automatically receives 25% so their lost chair time is respected, and the remaining 75% goes straight into the client's in-app ABE Wallet for their next booking.
  • What Happens If a Nail Tech Cancels? The client gets 100% credited to their wallet immediately plus a discount voucher toward their next booking with any tech on the platform.
Part 6 • Phase 2 Expansion & Our Roles

Where We Grow Next & How Aqeelah and Rio Work Together

Once our core nail booking app is running smoothly in CPT and JHB, we unlock Phase 2:

  • The "Stock-Up" Supply Store & Monthly Beauty Box (Phase 2) Techs will be able to buy nail gels, files, and supplies right inside the app using their pending earnings (zero cash needed upfront), and customers will be able to subscribe to our monthly curated Beauty Box with Golden Ticket prizes.
  • Aqeelah's Role (Brand, Quality & CPT) Aqeelah owns the beauty standards, CPT market research, recruiting our Founding Nail Techs, chatting with quiet CPT salons, and running social media. Every item on her checklist is built to grow her confidence step by step.
  • Rio's Role (App Engineering & JHB) Rio builds the iOS, Android, and Web apps, sets up the wallet and security systems, and handles JHB salons and nail techs on the ground.
